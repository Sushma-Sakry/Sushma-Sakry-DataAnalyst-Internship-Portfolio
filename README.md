**Focus:** Communicating business insights through a data-driven story and validating a business hypothesis statistically.

Key work included:
- Business objective definition
- Data storytelling
- Sales trend analysis
- Product and city performance analysis
- Business hypothesis formulation
- Welch independent two-sample t-test
- Interpretation of p-value and confidence interval

**Hypothesis:**  
Is average transaction value significantly different between Laptop and Mobile transactions?

**Result:**  
The Welch two-sample t-test produced a **p-value of 0.341**, which did not provide sufficient statistical evidence of a significant difference at the 5% significance level.

## Key Business Insights

The internship analysis provided several business-focused insights, including:

- Electronics was the highest-performing category by total sales.
- Laptop generated the highest total sales among the products.
- Mobile had the highest transaction count.
- Higher transaction volume did not always correspond to higher total sales.
- Sales performance varied across cities.
- Quarterly sales showed noticeable variation throughout 2025.
- Transaction value and quantity both contributed to overall sales performance.
- The statistical test did not provide sufficient evidence of a significant difference in average transaction value between Laptop and Mobile transactions.
